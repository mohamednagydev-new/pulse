## 1) Why THIS design (read once)

**The challenge: «تحدي الإلتزام ١٤ يوم» — 10 workouts in 14 days.**

- **Workout-count beats every other goal type for prizes.** Calories/water/lifts are
  self-typed numbers. A workout session takes real minutes, XP is throttled (8-min
  server rule), >4/day gets auto-flagged in the audit, and the proof-photo system
  attaches faces to numbers.
- **10 of 14 days is winnable by normal humans** — a rest-day margin keeps the
  middle of the pack alive (and the halfway streak-freeze gift fires at 5).
- **Prize mode `top3`** — a serious podium: the 500 makes the race real, and TWO
  200s mean second and third are worth fighting for to the last day.

**Prize split (total 900):**

## 3) Why the results can't be fake (the layered system — all already built)

| Layer | What it stops |
|---|---|
| 8-min XP throttle (server) | tap-spamming workout completions |
| Audit auto-flags: >4 workouts/day, >800 XP/day, account created mid-challenge | volume cheating & fresh multi-accounts |
| Calorie/day cap 6000 (server) | irrelevant here (goal = workouts) but closes the side door |
| **Proof check-ins 📸** | numbers with no human behind them — proofs are public in the room, so the community itself watches |
| **Stated max-2/day rule** | grinding 10 sessions in 2 days — you enforce it in review from the audit's per-day numbers |
| **/admin/challenge-audit** | the decision screen: top-10 fingerprints (XP/day, workouts/day, proof count, account age) with ⚠ flags |
| **Announced human review** | the biggest deterrent is that everyone KNOWS a human checks before money moves |

**Verification ritual (day after it ends, ~10 minutes):**
1. Open **Admin → Challenge audit** → pick the challenge.
2. Green-check winners with proofs → pay.
3. ⚠-flagged winner → message them: «مبروك! قبل تسليم الجايزة ابعتلنا صورة من
   تمرين النهارده أو فيديو قصير وانت بتستخدم التطبيق» — an honest winner answers
   in minutes; a cheater disappears.
4. Disqualified? The podium engine already announced? **No** — the engine waits for
   the morning after; do the audit BEFORE 10:00 or simply announce corrections in
   the same feed post's comments. (For big prizes: set endsOn one day earlier than
   the announced end so you always review before the auto-podium fires.)
