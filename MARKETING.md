## 0. The one-line positioning

> Competitive research behind this section: **[MARKET-RESEARCH.md](MARKET-RESEARCH.md)**.
> Real competitor set: **ElCoach** (Cairo, funded, bilingual, freemium — the one that matters),
> **Fitlov/JEFF** (Gulf, paid per session), **Nike Training Club** (free but not Egyptian),
> **Hevy/Strong/Fitbod** (loggers; capped free tiers or $96/yr).

**THE STATEMENT — use this verbatim, it is the whole strategy in one sentence:**
> **EN:** PULSE is the only fitness app that asks what hurts **before** it tells you what to do — written in Egyptian, free with no paywall, and it re-checks your plan every four weeks. No app store, no subscription, no excuses.
> **AR:** PULSE هو التطبيق الوحيد اللي بيسألك بيوجعك إيه **قبل** ما يقولك اعمل إيه — مكتوب بالمصري، مجاني من غير أي اشتراك، وبيراجع خطتك كل ٤ أسابيع. من غير ستور، من غير اشتراك، من غير أعذار.

**Short form (bios, ads, the one people repeat):**
> **EN:** The fitness app that explains itself. Egyptian. Free. Actually free.
> **AR:** التطبيق اللي بيقولك هي كده ليه. مصري. مجاني. مجاني بجد.

### The five claims only we can make

Lead with **one**. Never stack more than two in a post. Each one is checked against every competitor in the research doc — these are the sentences they physically cannot say back.

| # | The claim | Say it like this (AR) | Why they can't copy it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The plan tells you why** | «الخطة بتقولك هي كده ليه — مش بس بتقولك اعمل إيه» | Their AI can't explain itself. Ours was built rule-first on purpose |
| **2** | **It works around what hurts** | «ركبتك بتوجعك؟ الخطة بتتغير — مش بتستبعدك» | Everyone else ignores injuries or charges you for a human |
| **3** | **Free means free** | «مجاني ١٠٠٪ — مش "جرّب ٣ مرات" ولا "المدفوع أحسن"» | Fitbod = 3 workouts then $96/yr. Hevy = 4 routines. ElCoach gates its best work |
| **4** | **Egyptian, not translated** | «مكتوب بالمصري — مش مترجم» | Global apps can't buy it; Gulf apps won't bother with the dialect |
| **5** | **It re-checks itself** | «كل ٤ أسابيع بنراجع خطتك — لأن جسمك بيتغير» | Fitbod adapts the *workout*. Nobody adapts the *plan* and tells you it did |

**Two more that are ours, for when the format allows a longer line:**
- **No store, no download, ~1 MB.** «من غير ستور ومن غير تحميل — دوس اللينك وابدأ» — every competitor needs a 60–200 MB install first. On mobile data in Egypt, that is the funnel.
- **Zero commission, both ways.** «المدرب بياخد فلوسه كلها — إحنا مش بناخد عمولة» — Fitlov's entire model is the opposite.

### 🚫 Banned phrases

These are all true and all worthless, because every competitor says them. Using one costs you the hook:
> ~~"personalised plans"~~ · ~~"AI-powered"~~ · ~~"track your progress"~~ · ~~"all in one place"~~ · ~~"achieve your goals"~~ · ~~"خطة مخصصة ليك"~~ · ~~"حقق أهدافك"~~

Replace the generic with the specific every time: not "personalised plan" → **"nine questions, and it tells you why"**. Not "all in one place" → **"gym, yoga, kitchen and your coach — one link, no store"**.

### The soft version (for community posts, not ads)
> **EN:** PULSE is your pocket gym buddy — workouts, yoga, recipes and a community that keeps you going. 100% free.
> **AR:** PULSE هو صاحبك في التمرين — تمارين، يوجا، أكل صحي، ومجتمع يشدّ من ضهرك. مجاني بالكامل.

**Tone:** warm, funny, a little tough-love. Never shaming. Talk like a friend at the gym, not a brand.

## 4. TIKTOK / REELS SCRIPTS (the growth channel — prioritize this)

> **Rule:** the first 1.5 seconds decide everything. Start mid-action, no intros, no "hi guys".

**Script A — "POV" (highest reach format)**
```
0-2s  [Text on screen] لما تقرر تبدأ تتمرن بس مش عارف تبدأ منين
2-6s  [Screen record] فتح PULSE → خريطة العضلات → دوس على الصدر
6-12s [Screen record] التمارين بتظهر بالشرح والحركة
12-15s [Text] كله مجاني • اللينك في البايو
```

**Script B — "3 mistakes" (saves + shares)**
```
0-2s  "٣ أخطاء بتخليك متتقدمش في الجيم"
2-6s  خطأ ١: بتغيّر البرنامج كل أسبوع
6-10s خطأ ٢: مش بتسجّل أوزانك
10-14s خطأ ٣: بتتمرن لوحدك من غير حد يشدك
14-17s "PULSE بيحلّ التلاتة… مجاناً"
```

**Script C — Transformation / streak flex**
```
0-2s  [Show streak screen] "٣٠ يوم متواصلين 🔥"
2-8s  quick cuts of workouts + the app's streak counter climbing
8-12s "مش محتاج موهبة… محتاج بس متقطعش"
12-15s CTA
```

**Script D — Trend-jacking**
Take whatever audio is trending in Egypt this week and overlay a fitness caption. Check TikTok's Discover tab every Sunday and build 2 posts around top sounds.

**Script E — Duet/Stitch bait**
```
"قولي أكلتك المفضلة… وأنا أقولك نسختها الصحية 👇"
```
(Then reply to comments with video responses — each reply is a new post.)

## 7. LAUNCH PLAN (90 days)

### Phase 0 — Week -1 (before you post anything)
- [ ] Profiles set up on all 3 platforms with identical handle + bio
- [ ] 15 posts pre-made in Canva (so you never miss a day)
- [ ] App stocked: **20–30 reels approved**, onboarding images uploaded, 10+ recipe covers
- [ ] Ask 10 friends to install and post their first streak — you need social proof on day 1

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2: **Launch**
- Post the 7-launch-series posts + 1 TikTok/day
- Run the **7-day challenge** (comment "جاهز")
- Personally reply to **every single comment** (this is what builds the first 500 followers)
- Goal: 500 followers, 200 installs

### Phase 2 — Weeks 3–6: **Prove value**
- Shift to 40% quick-value content
- Start the FB group, daily rituals
- Feature 3 real users per week (their streaks/PRs)
- Launch **referral push**: "اعزم صاحبك — الاتنين تاخدوا 🧊" (built into the app)
- Goal: 2,000 followers, 1,000 installs, 30% weekly retention

### Phase 3 — Weeks 7–12: **Scale what works**
- Double down on your top-3 performing formats (check analytics — kill the rest)
- Partner with **5 micro-influencers** (5k–50k followers, Egyptian fitness/health) — free app, no cash: ask for 1 honest reel each
- Launch the **monthly season** publicly (the app auto-creates them) — "تحدي الشهر بدأ 🏆"
- Start collecting testimonials + before/afters
- Goal: 10,000 followers, 5,000 installs

## 8b. ANSWERING "HOW ARE YOU DIFFERENT FROM ___?"

You will get this in comments and DMs from day one. Answer fast, answer specific, never trash the competitor — naming them respectfully makes you sound confident, and it borrows their search traffic.

| They say | You say (AR) | You say (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| **"ده زي ElCoach"** | «ElCoach تطبيق كويس. الفرق إن اللي بيدفعوا فيه عندنا مجاني — والخطة عندنا بتقولك هي كده ليه، وبتتظبط لو عندك إصابة.» | "ElCoach is a good app. The difference: what they charge for, we give away — and our plan explains itself and works around injuries." |
| **"في Nike Training Club مجاني"** | «آه ومجاني فعلاً. بس مش بالمصري، ومفيهوش مدربين مصريين ولا وصفات أكل مصري ولا مجتمع تتمرن معاه.» | "It is, genuinely. But it's not in Egyptian, and there are no Egyptian coaches, no local recipes and no community." |
| **"أنا بستخدم Hevy/Strong"** | «دول مسجّلين تمارين ممتازين — بس مش بيعملولك خطة. استخدمهم معانا مش بدالنا.» | "Those are great loggers — they don't build you a plan. Use them alongside us, not instead." |
| **"هتبقوا مدفوعين بعدين"** | «لأ. الفلوس بتيجي من الشركاء والجيمات والمتاجر اللي بيوصلوا للناس من خلالنا — مش من جيبك.» | "No. Revenue comes from partners, gyms and stores reaching you — not from your pocket." |
| **"مفيش تطبيق على الستور"** | «مش محتاج ستور. دوس اللينك، وبعدين 'Add to Home Screen' — بيبقى تطبيق عندك بحجم أقل من صورة واحدة.» | "You don't need one. Open the link, tap Add to Home Screen — it becomes an app, smaller than a single photo." |
