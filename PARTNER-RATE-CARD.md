## Read this first (internal note — delete before sending)

**Every number below is a placeholder until you have traffic.** Pricing an audience
you cannot yet measure is how you end up either giving it away or being laughed out
of the room. The structure is right and it is what you actually sell; the figures
need one month of real numbers behind them.

Two rules that matter more than the prices:

1. **The free tier is not charity.** A directory with 4 gyms is worthless to users,
   so nobody pays for placement in it. Give away basic listings until you have 20+
   venues, then start charging for position. Density first, revenue second.
2. **Sell what you can prove.** Every placement below is instrumented — `views`,
   `contacts`, `redeems`, and real `Lead` rows with a name and phone number. That
   evidence is the product. Never quote a price for something you cannot report on.

## 2. Leads — the one they will actually pay for

A lead form on a partner's profile ("book a free trial", "book a tour"). The user
fills in name + phone; it lands in your admin and you hand it over.

| Model | Price (EGP) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Per lead** | **50 – 80** | Recommended. They only pay for a real name and a real number |
| **Monthly bundle — 30 leads** | **1,800** | ~60/lead, invoiced up front |
| **Monthly bundle — 100 leads** | **5,000** | ~50/lead |

**Set the price against what a member is worth to them, not against what feels
fair.** A gym at 600 EGP/month keeping a member ~8 months is ~4,800 EGP of revenue.
If one in five leads joins, each lead is worth ~960 EGP to them. **80 EGP is cheap
and you should say so out loud in the meeting.**

**Rules that keep this honest:**
- No charge for a duplicate number inside 90 days
- No charge for an unreachable number, if they tell you within 7 days
- They see the count in real time; you invoice against the same number

## 7. What you send them every month

One page, and it is what justifies the renewal:

- Profile views
- Contact taps (WhatsApp / call / directions) — **the number that matters**
- Coupon reveals
- Leads delivered, with dates
- How they rank against the city average

All of it comes off the existing counters. Never send a report with a metric you had
to estimate.

## 8. The first five partners

Do not sell to them. **Give them six months free** in exchange for:

1. Permission to use their name and logo in the next pitch
2. A short quote you can put in the deck
3. Honest feedback on what the profile is missing

You need proof more than you need 4,000 EGP. The sixth conversation is much easier
when the first five are already live, and "here are five gyms already using it" ends
the objection that this is vapourware.

## 10. Objections, and the honest answer

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "How many users do you have?" | Give the real number. Inflating it ends the relationship the first time they check their own leads |
| "Facebook is cheaper" | Facebook sells reach. We hand you a name and a phone number of someone who was looking at gyms in your city. Different thing |
| "Can I pay per member who joins?" | No — we cannot see inside your CRM, so neither of us could verify it. Per lead is the closest honest version |
| "Send me the leads first" | Ten free, then we talk. Never more |
| "Is it exclusive?" | Only the category sponsorship. Everything else is open, and we will tell you who else is in your city if you ask |

## Currency note

Prices are quoted in **EGP** and every partner in the app is billed in **their own
country's currency** — the app shows a Saudi gym's prices in SAR and an Egyptian
gym's in EGP, with no conversion, because conversion shows people a number they will
never be asked to pay. For partners outside Egypt, re-quote this card in local
currency rather than converting these figures; the right price in Riyadh is not the
FX of the right price in Cairo.
