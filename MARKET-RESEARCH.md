# PULSE — Market Research & Competitive Position
**Scope compared:** bilingual (EN + Egyptian Arabic) fitness content platform, free, PWA, viewer-only commerce.
**Verified against the build on 2026-08-04:** 18 programs · 99 lessons (99 with video) · 85 exercises (85 with video) · 17 muscle groups · 120 articles · 72 recipes · 37 challenges · 36 badges · 8 coaches · 69 data models · 44 Arabic content columns.

**Read this correctly.** The money in MENA is concentrated in Gulf markets (Saudi, UAE, Qatar) where subscriptions convert. Egypt is a *volume* market, not an ARPU market — the same reports note that Egypt defaults to free, ad-supported access. **A subscription-first product in Egypt is fighting the market. A free product monetised through partners is running with it.** Your no-subscription decision is not a compromise; it is the correct read of this specific market.

### 2c. What the whole field converges on

Across all of them, three defaults repeat: **(1)** the app asks 2–3 questions at signup and never asks again, **(2)** the plan arrives with no explanation, **(3)** getting anything adaptive costs money. That triple gap is the opening.

## 4. What is genuinely ours — the five defensible claims

Ordered by how hard they are to copy. Only these belong in the first line of an ad.

**1. The plan tells you why.** Nine questions in, and what comes back is a level, a program, training days, cautions — *and the reasoning attached*. Not one competitor in this set does this. Copying it means rebuilding your engine to be explainable, which most AI-plan products structurally cannot do.
> **AR hook:** «الخطة بتقولك هي كده ليه — مش بس بتقولك اعمل إيه»

**2. It works around what hurts.** The intake asks what already hurts. Two or more limitations drop the starting level. Contraindications are attached to exercises. Prenatal content never surfaces to someone who did not ask for it. Everyone else either ignores injuries or charges you for a human to handle them.
> **AR hook:** «ركبتك بتوجعك؟ الخطة بتتغير — مش بتستبعدك»

**3. It re-checks itself.** Every four weeks it measures whether the plan actually worked, then proposes restart / reduce / deload / increase — with a 7-day cooldown so it does not thrash. Miss sessions and it adjusts instead of guilting you. Fitbod adapts the *workout*; nobody adapts the *plan* and then tells you it did.
> **AR hook:** «كل ٤ أسابيع بنراجع خطتك — لأن جسمك بيتغير»

**4. Free means free.** Not 3 routines. Not 3 workouts. Not "basic plans free, the useful ones paid." Everything — plan engine, 99 lessons, 85 exercises, 120 articles, 72 recipes, coaches, leagues, live sessions. In a market where Fitbod is $96/yr and ElCoach gates its best work, this is the loudest single sentence you own.
> **AR hook:** «مجاني ١٠٠٪ — مش «جربه ٣ مرات» ولا «النسخة المدفوعة أحسن»»

**5. Egyptian, not translated.** Every string, article, recipe and coaching cue is hand-written in simple Egyptian — no machine translation, no stiff فصحى. 44 Arabic content columns, 18/18 programs and 120/120 articles translated. Recipes are Egyptian food. This is the one thing a global app cannot buy quickly and a Gulf app will not bother doing for the Egyptian dialect.
> **AR hook:** «مكتوب بالمصري — مش مترجم»

**Two more that are ours but harder to say in one line:**
- **No store, no download, ~1 MB.** On a mid-range Android on mobile data, PULSE is a link. Every competitor above needs a 60–200 MB store install first. Install-friction is the single biggest funnel leak in this market, and we do not have it.
- **Zero commission, both sides.** Coaches keep their client relationship; stores keep their sale; PULSE never touches the transaction. Fitlov's whole model is the opposite.

## 6. Positioning statement — use this verbatim

> **PULSE is the only fitness app that asks what hurts before it tells you what to do — written in Egyptian, free with no paywall, and it re-checks your plan every four weeks. No app store, no subscription, no excuses.**

**AR:**
> **PULSE هو التطبيق الوحيد اللي بيسألك بيوجعك إيه قبل ما يقولك اعمل إيه — مكتوب بالمصري، مجاني من غير أي اشتراك، وبيراجع خطتك كل ٤ أسابيع. من غير ستور، من غير اشتراك، من غير أعذار.**

**Message hierarchy — lead with #1, never stack more than two:**
1. The plan explains itself · 2. It works around injuries · 3. Free means free · 4. Egyptian, not translated · 5. It re-checks itself every 4 weeks

**Claims to retire from marketing** (true, but every competitor says them, so they cost you the hook): "personalised plans", "AI-powered", "track your progress", "all in one place", "achieve your goals".

## 7. The one strategic risk

**ElCoach is the real competitor** — Cairo-based, funded, bilingual, 350k downloads, and already doing local food culture. Where they beat us: distribution, funding, app-store presence, nutrition depth. Where we beat them: **their best work is behind a paywall and ours is not**, they explain nothing, they do not handle injuries, and they have no community/gamification/local-commerce layer.

**So the wedge is:** do not out-feature them. Out-*honest* them. Everything they charge for, we give away and explain. Then monetise the sponsors who want access to the audience that creates — which is a business they cannot copy without abandoning their own subscription revenue.
