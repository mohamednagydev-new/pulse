## Suggested first-wave order

1. **El-Marg / Matareya / Hadayek El-Kobba / Ain Shams / Helwan** — smallest neighborhood gyms, owner answers the WhatsApp himself, zero competing SaaS pitches; Vandam, elsory, Amir, Private, Fit Club, NitroGym, X.fit are all direct mobile lines.
2. **Haram/Faisal + Shubra El-Kheima belt** — high-density working-class gym market (ARENA, Trends, Gymnation, Viking, BOB, Muscle Station, Golden); retention leaderboard pitch lands well.
3. **October/Zayed mid-market** (ZERO FAT, Volt, Bahgat, COVA, T2, SHIFT) — multi-branch small chains; one yes = several branches.
4. **New Cairo / Maadi premium independents** (Titans, Glory, Revive, First Gym, Fighter) — have websites, most likely to value an app-based toolkit.
5. **Page-only rows** — Facebook/Instagram DM referencing their recent posts.
