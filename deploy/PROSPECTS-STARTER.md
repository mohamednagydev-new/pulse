# PULSE — Starter B2B Prospect List (Egypt)

**What this is:** a starter outreach list of mid-size local Egyptian gyms and independent fitness coaches for PULSE's partner program (gym retention toolkit / free coach platform). Compiled from live web research on **2026-08-24**.

**Rules followed:**
- Every row was found at a real public source (gym's own Facebook page, its own website, or a public business directory that mirrors the gym's listing). Nothing is fabricated; the exact source URL for every prospect is in the Sources table at the bottom.
- Only publicly listed **business** contact channels were collected (numbers/emails the business itself publishes for customer contact).
- International chains (Gold's, Fitness First, World Gym, Anytime, Powerhouse) were excluded — decisions there are corporate.
- Businesses with no public phone AND no public email are NOT in the CSV; the active-looking ones are in the "page-only leads" table for manual Facebook/Instagram outreach.

**Honest caveats:**
- As expected, **emails are scarce** — Egyptian gyms are overwhelmingly Facebook + phone/WhatsApp businesses. Only 4 rows have an email. Lead with WhatsApp and the page-only table, not email blasts.
- Rows marked **[V]** in the Sources table came from a page I fetched and read directly. Unmarked rows came from the search-result snippet of the cited listing (directory or Facebook "About" section) — spot-check the number on the linked page before high-volume outreach.
- Most numbers are mobile (01x…) and almost certainly WhatsApp-capable; normalized to +20 international format where the source showed a mobile. Landlines kept as listed.

## Suggested first-wave order

1. **Tanta + Mansoura gyms** — smallest markets, owners answer their own WhatsApp, PULSE's leaderboard-TV pitch is most novel there.
2. **Alexandria mid-size gyms** (Premiere, Summit, Eagle, Reform, Planet) — all mobile numbers, active Facebook pages.
3. **Coaches with direct lines** (Elfadaly, Almadawy, Horany, HIT Egypt) — free client-management platform is a zero-risk pitch.
4. **Page-only coaches** — Facebook DM referencing their recent posts; they are the exact profile PULSE's coach tools serve.
