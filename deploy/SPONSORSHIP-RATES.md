# PULSE — Sponsorship rate card (internal)

For the human closer (you), not the AI — the growth agent keeps pitching the founding
"prize-only" deal; this card is what you negotiate from when a sponsor asks "what's next"
or when founding slots run out. Prices in EGP, launch-stage (revisit when MAU grows).

## What a sponsor actually gets (all live in-product today)

| Placement | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Sponsored challenge** | Their name on the challenge card, «برعاية X» banner INSIDE the challenge room (seen daily for 30 days by every participant), Home auto-banner while the prize window runs, winners-announcement post with their brand, permanent Wall of Champions entry |
| **Deals slot** | Their offer in /deals with a WhatsApp buy button — zero commission |
| **Featured partner** | Pinned placement in the partners/stores directory |
| **Live class sponsor** | "الحصة برعاية X" on a flagship live class + shout-out in the stream |

## Packages

### 1 · Founding sponsor — FREE (the current pitch)
- They provide the **prize only** (product bundles, vouchers — EGP 2–5k value).
- Gets: full sponsored-challenge placement + winners post.
- In exchange: they announce the challenge on their own page + logo/quote for ours.
- Limit: first 3–5 sponsors, then the paid tiers below. Renewal terms locked in writing.

### 2 · Sponsored challenge — EGP 5,000–15,000 / challenge
- Everything in the placement table row 1.
- Price by prize window (2 vs 4 weeks) and season (Ramadan premium).
- Sponsor still provides the prizes on top (that's the credibility of the challenge).

### 3 · Monthly bundle — EGP 10,000–25,000 / month
- 1 sponsored challenge + deals slot + featured directory pin + 1 sponsored live class.
- The "own the month" package — one sponsor per month, exclusivity per category
  (one supplement brand at a time, one water brand at a time…).

### 4 · Affiliate/code deal — revenue share, no fee
- Store gives a PULSE discount code; we take 5–10% of tracked sales.
- Zero-risk starter for stores that won't pay upfront yet.

## Rules (protects the product)
- Max ONE sponsored challenge live at a time — scarcity is the price floor.
- Sponsor content must fit brand rules (no miracle claims, no competitor bashing,
  supplements must be legal/registered — نقول لأ للمنتجات المشبوهة).
- Prizes must be real and delivered; we announce winners with receipts (proof posts).
- Never sell: push notifications, the workout screen, the food logger. Those stay clean.
