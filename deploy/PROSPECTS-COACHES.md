# PULSE Prospects — Egyptian Coaches, Trainers, Dietitians & Fitness Influencers (Deep List)

Compiled 2026-08-24 via live web sweep. Pitch: free client-management platform + personal invite link (every signup becomes THEIR client).

Scope & rules:
- **All names are NEW** — every row was checked against `PROSPECTS-STARTER.md` and `PROSPECTS-PARTNERS.md` (Elfadaly, Almadawy, Horany, Kaoud, HIT, Miracle Nassif, Aly Mazhar, Dietitian Yassin, XFIT Tribe, KAJ, Farah Nofal, Dr. Shorouk + the ~25 page-only coaches already listed there are excluded here).
- Segments covered: online-coaching trainers with their own sites/apps, **women's fitness & pre/postnatal coaches (priority — most rows)**, dietitians/nutritionists with booking pages, calisthenics/CrossFit, Egyptian YouTube fitness channels, yoga studio founders.
- Nothing fabricated. Only publicly listed business contacts. Rows without a public email/phone are included with their public page URL as the DM channel (this is the norm — the Egyptian coach market runs on Instagram DM; only 1 public email surfaced in this sweep, matching the pattern seen in the sibling sweeps).
- `notes` column = ONE canonical page URL per row (the DM/outreach channel). Every row's origin is in the Sources table.
- Sweep constraints: general web search engines (DuckDuckGo/Bing/Ecosia/Mojeek) were bot-blocked during this session, so hunting was done by directly fetching aggregator listicles (CairoGyms 2025/2026 editions — different names than the ones the sibling file already took), the Heepsy Cairo YouTube ranking, Vezeeta's nutrition directory, and individual Instagram profiles / coach websites. TikTok-only creators could not be surfaced this session (TikTok blocks bot fetches); TikTok presence is noted where found via coach sites (e.g. Karim Gabriel is @kg.karimgabriel on TikTok).
- Deliberately excluded after verification: Diala Jamaleddin (Syrian, not Egyptian per her own bio), Nervana Shaheen (UAE-based per bio link), Manal Rostom (Nike-tier, audience far above the 5k-200k band), Shankoty (228K, just over band), platform apps that compete with PULSE (ElCoach, TrainTrack, iCoach, iTrainer, Airon, Racemate).
- Caveats: Samar Aboul Yazid's FemPWR lists a UAE company location but was featured in the Egyptian CairoGyms guide — verify nationality on first contact. Karim Ebied's BiA app IG is @brothersinarms_dxb (Dubai-branded app, Egyptian coach at LA7). Follower counts are stated only where verified by a direct profile fetch.
