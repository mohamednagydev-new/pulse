# PULSE — Partner Acquisition Plan (coaches · gyms · stores · sponsors)

*The one document that answers: how do we reach them, what do they get, what do we
say, and what do they pay. Scripts live in PARTNERS-PITCH.md, prices in
PARTNER-RATE-CARD.md, phasing in GROWTH-ROADMAP.md — this stitches them into one
operating plan.*

---

## 0. The core insight — sell the tool, not the traffic

With ~500 users we cannot sell an audience, and we should never pretend to.
What we CAN sell today, honestly, is **software they would otherwise pay for or
do by hand** — and every partner type has a version of that:

| Partner | Their pain today | What PULSE replaces — value with ZERO traffic from us |
|---|---|---|
| Coach | WhatsApp + Excel + screenshots of workouts | Client roster, assigned programs, progress dashboards, invite link, group live sessions — a coaching SaaS, free |
| Gym | Members get bored, quit at month 2; no digital presence | Member app + gym leaderboard + TV screen on the floor + challenges for their EXISTING members — a retention tool |
| Store | No cheap way to reach fitness buyers | Profile + deals placement + sponsored challenge visibility — branding that compounds as we grow |
| Sponsor | Wants engagement, not impressions | A challenge with their name ON the mechanic people sweat through daily for 30 days |

The pitch is never "we have users for you." It is **"this makes the people you
already have worth more — and everyone who joins through you is yours."**

## 1. What each partner owns in the product (all live today)

### Coach
- Verified profile in the public coach directory (photo, headline, specialties)
- **Personal invite link** — anyone who signs up through it lands pre-connected
  as their client. *Their* client, on their roster, forever.
- Client dashboards: workouts done, streaks, weight trend, quiet-client alerts
- Programs: public (marketing) or clients-only (paid programming stays private)
- Group live sessions they host: shared timer, synced video, room chat
- Coach requests inbox — inbound clients from the directory

### Gym
- Partner page: name, map pin, photos, hours, facilities, WhatsApp button
- **Gym invite code** — members who join with it are attached to the gym.
  Owner sees his people, his numbers. Owned users, not borrowed ones.
- Gym-only leaderboard + **TV leaderboard screen** (`/tv/<id>`) for the floor —
  the demo trick that closes meetings
- Owner analytics: active members, workouts this week, top performers
- Lead form on the profile ("book a free trial") → lands in our admin → handed over

### Store
- Profile page + product/deal placements in the Deals section
- Coupon codes we push in challenges ("finish week 1 → 15% at X")
- Later: store category sponsorship (supplements, equipment)

### Sponsor
- **Sponsored challenge**: their name + logo + link on a 30-day challenge
  mechanic (sponsorName/sponsorUrl are built in), prize award at the end,
  winners announcement post with their brand
- Banner placement in-app + event listing if they run physical events

### Everyone
- A public page URL they can put in their Instagram bio / Facebook page
- The self-service partner portal (own page editing, leads pipeline)
- A monthly one-page report (rate card §7): views, leads, members, workouts

## 2. The pitch, per type (scripts in PARTNERS-PITCH.md — this is the spine)

- **Coach:** «إنت بتدير عملاءك من واتساب وإكسل. إحنا بنديك نظام كامل ببلاش —
  ولينك خاص بيك: أي حد يسجل منه يبقى عميلك إنت، مش بتوعنا.»
  *You run clients from WhatsApp. We give you the system, free — and your link
  makes every signup YOUR client.*
- **Gym:** «مش بنبيعلك أعضاء — بنخلي أعضاءك الحاليين يكملوا. شاشة ليدربورد في
  الجيم، تحديات باسمك، وكل عضو يدخل بكودك يبقى محسوب ليك.»
  *We don't sell you members — we keep yours from quitting. And the TV screen
  demo does the talking: open /tv on their television in the meeting.*
- **Store:** «أول ما حد يخلص تحدي، الجايزة كوبون منك. الناس اللي بتكسب بتحبك.»
- **Sponsor:** «مش إعلان يتقلب — اسمك على التحدي اللي الناس بتعرق فيه ٣٠ يوم،
  وإعلان الفائزين باسمك.»

**The one rule in every meeting: never claim traffic we don't have.** Say the
real number if asked, then pivot: "that's exactly why founding partners get it
free — you grow as we grow, at a price that never goes up on you."

## 3. The offer — my recommendation (vs. "3 months free for first 10")

Three-tier founding structure, per category, per city:

| Tier | Who | Offer | In exchange for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anchor** (2–3 names max) | The gym/brand whose logo sells the next ten | **6 months free Featured** + TV setup visit | Logo + quote in the deck, code pushed to ALL their members, a case-study number we can publish |
| **Founding 10** | Next 10 per category | **3 months free Verified** (gyms/stores) — coaches are free anyway, their version is "Founding Coach" badge + permanent featured slot | Logo + quote, invite code actually distributed (not just accepted) |
| **Everyone after** | — | Rate card as written: Basic free / Verified 1,500 / Featured 4,000 EGP, leads 50–80 EGP | — |

Why this beats a flat "3 months for first 10": the *first two or three* logos are
worth far more than the rest — overpay them with 6 months, because "Gold's Gym
is on it" closes doors ten cheap deals can't. And **coaches should stay free
permanently at the core tier** — coaches are supply, not customers: every coach
brings clients (users) with them. Charge coaches only later, for a Pro tier
(featured placement, client-cap lift), and only after ~50 active coaches.

**Two conditions on every free deal, non-negotiable:**
1. The code/link actually goes out to their audience (a founding partner who
   never posts the code is a logo, not a partner — and we need the users).
2. Renewal price is locked in writing at signup ("your price never increases
   as long as you stay") — this is what makes "free now" feel safe, not cheap.

## 4. The connection roadmap (aligned with GROWTH-ROADMAP phases)

**Weeks 1–2 — Coaches (target: 5 active).** Cheapest to close, they carry users
in. Source: Facebook fitness groups where they already answer questions,
Instagram trainers with 1–10k followers (big enough to have clients, small
enough to answer DMs). WhatsApp script → 10-min screen-share demo → create their
profile WITH them on the call → first program uploaded before the call ends.

**Weeks 3–6 — Gyms (target: 5, one Anchor).** Source: the gyms our existing
users already train in (ask in-app: "which gym do you go to?"), plus every gym
within delivery distance of your area. Walk in at off-peak, ask for the owner,
demo = open the TV leaderboard on your phone, then on THEIR television.
Founding offer on the spot, one-page agreement, set up the code before leaving.

**Weeks 7–10 — Stores + first sponsor (target: 2 stores, 1 sponsored challenge).**
Stores: supplement shops near partner gyms — their customers are literally our
users. Sponsor: local brand for ONE challenge where the sponsor's only cost is
the prize (protein tub, smartwatch). We eat the fee for the first one — the
winners-announcement post with their brand is the case study that prices the
second one.

**Week 10+ — Convert.** Founding periods start expiring; the monthly report
they've been receiving is the renewal pitch. Anyone who won't pay stays on
free Basic — density keeps the directory alive either way.

**Weekly cadence (roughly 4 hrs/week):** Mon — send 10 first-messages · Wed —
demos booked from replies · Fri — follow-ups + send monthly reports + update
the pipeline in admin Leads.

## 5. Numbers to watch (admin → Analytics + Leads)

- Partners live per category (target: 5 coaches / 5 gyms / 2 stores by week 10)
- % of partners whose code was actually used ≥ 10 times (the real health metric)
- Users attributed to partner codes vs. organic (the "owned users" proof)
- Leads delivered vs. leads charged-for (keep the honesty rules of the rate card)
- Renewal rate at end of founding period — the only number that proves the model
