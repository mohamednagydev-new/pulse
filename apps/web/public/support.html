Common questions

  • How do I reset my password? — On the login screen tap "Forgot password" and follow the email link.
  • How do I delete my account? — In the app: Settings → Delete my account. Or see pulse.geddo.online/delete-account.
  • Is PULSE really free? — Yes. No subscriptions, no purchases, no paid tier.
  • How do I change the app language? — Arabic/English toggle is at the top of the menu, and on the login screen.
  • Notifications aren't arriving? — Allow notifications for PULSE in your device Settings, and check they're enabled inside the app under Settings.

In-app help

The app includes a full guide under Menu → How it works (pulse.geddo.online/help), and in-app Support chat for signed-in users.
